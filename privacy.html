We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal information, including but not limited to:

Third-Party Services

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. It only uses Apple’s native system APIs for core functionality, such as iCloud sync, which do not share your personal data with us.
